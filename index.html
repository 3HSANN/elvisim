ELVISim is the NI ELVIS II+ workstation in your browser. Build the circuit on a breadboard, measure it with the DMM and oscilloscope, and see exactly what the real board would show, mistakes included.

Real breadboard wiring

Drag wires and parts into holes. Hover any hole to see its node; Check circuit tells you which part is in the wrong row.

DMM like the real one

Plug leads into VΩ, COM and A. Ammeter across a supply? The supply trips, just like in the lab.

Scope with Edge trigger

Function generator, two channels, a trigger type and slope, and a separate channel for each cursor, set up like ELVISmx.

Numbers you can trust

Why it exists

Entry · 17 Nov

The ELE 211 practical exam isn't hard on theory. It's hard on procedure under time pressure: which row is which node, where the red probe goes when you break a branch, why the scope trace won't sit still.

You only get to practise that on the real bench during lab hours. So I turned our practice-session script into a bench I could run at home, wrong turns included, and it spread through the class that week.
